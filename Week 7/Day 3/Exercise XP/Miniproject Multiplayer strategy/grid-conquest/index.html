      How to play

      • Players take turns moving one square up, down, left or right.
      • Rocks 🪨 and the other player block your way.
      • Step onto the enemy base 🏰 to win.
      • Standing next to the enemy base? Press Attack to capture it, even if the enemy is sitting on it.

      Players

        Arrow keys or WASD also move. Click a highlighted square to step there.
